# Elite: account deletion and privacy pages

The public account-deletion page and privacy policy for Elite, my habit app for iOS and Android. Elite is in private
testing and not in the app stores yet.

Two static pages with no scripts and no outside requests, served by Vercel:

- [`index.html`](https://elite-account-deletion.vercel.app/): how to delete your Elite account and data.
- [`privacy.html`](https://elite-account-deletion.vercel.app/privacy.html): what the app collects, why, and who
  processes it.

`privacy.html` is edited here. `index.html` is copied from the private app repo, so change it there first.
