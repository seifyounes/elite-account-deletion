Elite Privacy Policy

Elite - Own your day · com.eliteapp.tracker · Effective August 15, 2026

Who we are

This policy covers the Elite mobile app ("Elite", "we", "us"). Elite is a habit tracker: you tell it what you want to do, it tells you the truth about whether you did it. This page explains what data the app collects, why, and what happens to it.

Questions about this policy or your data: seifyounes222@gmail.com.

What we collect

Everything below is stored under your account so the app can sync across your devices.

Your account

  • Email address, how you signed in (email, Google, or Apple), and an account ID.
  • Profile details you enter: display name, your goal, birth year, your estimate of daily wasted time, and your timezone.

Your content

  • Your ten WHYs - the personal reasons you write during onboarding.
  • Your habits, including names you write for custom habits, plus their category, difficulty, frequency, quantity targets, and how familiar you told us you are with each one.
  • Which habits you completed on which days, and logged quantities.
  • Daily memories (your journal line), daily mood, weekly plans, and warm-up activity.
  • Your answers to the in-app setup questions, and your progress through in-app guided flows.

What the app derives

  • Daily scores, streaks, XP, levels, ranks, badges, discipline-muscle stats, and your monthly leaderboard standing.

Purchases

  • Subscription status (product, active period) via RevenueCat. We never receive your card or payment details - payment is processed entirely by the App Store or Google Play.

Usage data

  • A small, fixed set of app-interaction events with numeric properties (for example load and strength values, and the app version), linked to your account ID. These events never include your custom habit names or any free-text you write.

How we use your data

  • To run the product: sync your data across devices, compute scores, streaks, XP and the rest of your progression.
  • To manage your subscription and unlock what you paid for.
  • To keep the service working and safe: rate limiting, abuse prevention, debugging.
  • To understand how the app is used and improve it, using the usage data above.
  • To reply when you contact us.
  • To comply with the law when we are legally required to.

We may also create aggregated or de-identified statistics that cannot identify you, and use them for any purpose.

Who we share data with

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers. We share it only with the service providers that run Elite:

  • Supabase - our database, authentication, and backend hosting. Your data is stored on servers in London, United Kingdom.
  • Google - Google sign-in, if you choose it.
  • Apple - Sign in with Apple if you choose it, and App Store billing.
  • RevenueCat - subscription management. It receives an app user ID and purchase information from the store, never your payment details.

We may disclose data if required by law, or to protect Elite's rights, safety, or users. If Elite is ever acquired or merged, your data may transfer to the new owner under this same policy.

What Elite does not do

  • No ads, and no advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or renting of your data - to anyone, ever.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone. The app does not request those permissions.
  • No AI. Your content is not sent to any AI service.
  • Reminders are generated on your device. Your habit data is not sent to any push-notification service.

Storage and security

All data travels encrypted (TLS). In the database, per-account access rules ensure your rows are readable only by your own signed-in account.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security - but Elite is built so that the most personal data stays under your account and nothing leaves without a reason listed on this page.

How long we keep data - and how to delete it

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, everything tied to it is permanently erased - profile, habits and their full history, WHYs, plans, memories, moods, AI usage records, and the analytics linked to your account. Deletion is immediate and there is no undo.

  • In the app: Settings → Account → Delete account.
  • Without the app: see the account deletion page, or email seifyounes222@gmail.com from your sign-up address - your data will be deleted within 30 days.

Residual copies may persist in routine backups for a short period before they are cycled out.

Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. Subscriptions are managed by the store you bought them from - cancel in the App Store or Google Play, or the store keeps billing you.

Your choices and rights

  • Almost everything Elite stores is visible and editable directly in the app - your profile, habits, WHYs, plans, memories, and moods.
  • Notifications are controlled in the app and in your device settings.
  • You can delete your account and all its data at any time, as described above.

Depending on where you live, the law may give you additional rights over your personal data - such as access, correction, deletion, or objection. Email us at seifyounes222@gmail.com and we will respond as the applicable law requires.

Children

Elite is not directed at children under 13, requires users to be at least 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we will delete it.

International use

Your data is stored in the United Kingdom and processed by the providers listed above, some of which operate in other countries (for example, Google in the United States). By using Elite you understand your data is processed in these locations.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Elite evolves. The effective date at the top always reflects the current version, and material changes will be announced in the app or on this page. Continuing to use Elite after a change takes effect means you accept the updated policy.
